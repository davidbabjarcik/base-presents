About

We create emotions.

We're a crew from Košice who love events and electronic music, and we want to push the scene further. Since 2021 we've been throwing student parties together with student associations: Chodbovica, the multi-stage indoor night at Jedlíkova 7, and Chillout, the free open-air at Jedlíkova 9 where a sunset set turns into a party.
