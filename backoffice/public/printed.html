Printed tickets

Generate tickets in advance, print them, give them to sellers. A printed ticket counts only when a seller marks it sold. Unsold ones are not in the totals and do not open the door.

1 · Generate tickets to print

Every ticket gets a running number (#0001, #0002…) and its own QR code. Then print the PDF: 10 tickets per A4 page, cut along the dashed lines.

2 · Give tickets to a seller

Only unsold tickets move. Choose “— nobody —” to take tickets back. Need a new seller account (e.g. policiasrj9)? Add it in Users with the role Seller.

Void lost or leftover tickets

A voided ticket can not be sold and does not open the door. Sold tickets are never voided.
